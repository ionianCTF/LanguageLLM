## Purpose

Write about religions, denominations, and believers accurately — avoiding
conflation, dysphemism, unnecessary gendering of the divine, and exclusionary
calendar/idiom defaults.

## When to use

Any content mentioning religion, religious groups, worship, or religious calendars.

## Rules

**R1 — Don't conflate categories.** Arab ≠ Muslim (and vice versa); nationality,
ethnicity, and religion are distinct. Route ethnicity references to
ethnicity-language.

**R2 — Terminology table (avoid → use):**

| Avoid | Use |
|---|---|
| BC/AD (year numbering) | BCE/CE (unless the publication's convention is BC/AD — consistency wins) |
| He/His/Father (default deity pronouns) | deity names (God, Allah, Yahweh…); non-specific: Parent, Creator; god/goddess lower-case for polytheistic contexts |
| Kingdom/Princedom (metaphoric) | domain, realm, sphere, house |
| Layman/Laywoman/Clergyman | layperson; minister, leader, ecclesiastic |
| Master/Son/Lord (ordinary Jesus references) | Teacher; Child (of God); Heir; Sovereign; advocacy exists for "The Human One" |
| pagan (lower-case, generic) | Pagan capitalised for revived religions (Wicca, Druidry) and Indigenous religions (Romuva, Voudun, Santeria) |
| Bible-basher, God-botherer | neutral description of the belief/practice |
| "to jew" (verb), bargain metaphors on Jewish identity | — antisemitic; never generate |

**R3 — Capitalisation discipline.**
- Lower-case god/goddess as common noun or polytheistic reference.
- Capitalise deistic proper nouns everywhere.
- Direct scripture quotation: preserve reverential capitalisation ('Him', 'His')
  verbatim (G5).
- Gendered deity pronouns outside quotes: apply one consistent convention.

**R4 — Gendered references to figures of worship:** ask whether they are required
(exegesis, quoting) or prejudicial default; prefer names/functions otherwise.

**R5 — Denominational nuance.** Sweeping generalisations ignore internal
differences; specify movement/school where relevant (Sunni/Shia, Orthodox/Reform,
Mahayana/Theravada).

**R6 — Hidden ableism check.** Idioms like "standing up for one's beliefs"
exclude; prefer "maintaining", "advocating", "holding to".

**R7 — Secular-inclusive phrasing.** Question BC/AD-style defaults in public-facing
material; question gendered idioms; ensure invitations/events accommodate
observance (dates, food) — see multimodal-inclusion events section.

## Guardrails

- G5: scripture and liturgy are quoted verbatim, including gendered deity language.
- G12: antisemitic/Islamophobic tropes are never generated; historical discussion
  requires explicit critical framing.

## Uncertainty

Convention conflicts (BC/AD vs BCE/CE; capitalized deity pronouns) are style-guide
decisions: follow the client's guide first; otherwise BCE/CE + consistent
capitalisation, flagged.
