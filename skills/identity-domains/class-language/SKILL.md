## Purpose

Describe socioeconomic circumstances without blame, deficit framing, or implicit
racial coding.

## When to use

Any content mentioning income, poverty, education level, housing status, or
social class.

## Rules

**R1 — Describe circumstance, not identity.** Deficit-based language focuses on
what people lack and implies blame ("poorly educated", "dropouts"). State the
situation neutrally.

**R2 — Terminology table (avoid → use):**

| Avoid | Use |
|---|---|
| high-school dropouts; poorly educated people | people without a high-school diploma; people without a university degree |
| the homeless | people experiencing homelessness |
| illegal immigrants; "illegals" | people without documents for legal immigration (route legal-status specifics through formal-legal-texts if binding text) |
| the poor; poor people | people with low incomes; people below the poverty threshold |
| welfare mothers | mothers who receive income support; mothers who receive [program] benefits |
| underclass | people experiencing poverty / marginalisation (name the mechanism) |
| unskilled worker | worker in [sector]; entry-level worker (all work involves skill) |

**R3 — Explicit descriptors prevent coding.** Socioeconomic terms have been used
to imply minority ethnic groups. When both dimensions are meant, state both
explicitly ("low-income African American communities") rather than letting one
term carry the other's stereotype.

**R4 — No judgemental comparatives (G10).** "Failing schools" → "schools with
low resourcing"; describe systems and conditions, not inherent deficiency.

**R5 — Intersectionality.** Poverty interacts with gender, age, disability, and
migrant background (e.g. older migrant women and energy poverty). Name
interactions when relevant instead of generic "the vulnerable".

## Guardrails

- G3: mention class only when relevant.
- G11: immigration status is sensitive — never speculate or expose it.

## Uncertainty

Program-specific benefit names vary by jurisdiction (TANF, EI, Universal Credit).
Use the neutral generic form unless the client supplies program names.
